Privacy Policy for beech

Last updated November 27, 2026. beech is made by Jenerator Labs. Contact: markjen@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, Apple, or Steam we receive your first name (or Steam persona name), an account identifier from that provider (such as your SteamID), and — for Google sign-in only, when Google confirms it — your email address. We do not receive your Apple or Steam email address, and we never receive passwords or payment details. When you play signed in we store your saved beaches (sand, placed items, beach name, and which real-world beach it is set on), your item inventory, stars, neighbour links, gifts, and your purchase records (store, transaction ID, product). We also receive three small automatic reports from the app: error messages (up to 500 characters, with no user ID attached), page-load timings, and diagnostic lifecycle events (such as app starts and sign-in or purchase outcomes, with device platform and app version), used only to fix bugs. If you turn on notifications, we store a push address for that device (from your browser's push service, Apple or Google), your device's time zone (for quiet hours) and which kinds of notification you want; it is deleted when you turn notifications off, sign out on that device, or delete your account, and it is used only to tell you about things in your inbox. Your location is used only on your device, once, if you tap "near me" in settings to pick the closest beach — it is never stored or sent to us.

When you order from the beech store (store.beech.games) we store the order: what you bought, the price, the email address, name, shipping address and phone number you give Stripe's checkout, its tracking details, and — for a "your beach" print — a copy of that beach as it was when you ordered. Store orders need no account; to print your own beach you sign in with Google or Apple, and the order is linked to your account.

When you submit an item on Beechshore we store your upload, any preview, your proposal, the terms you accepted, the review decision, and any creator credits linked to your account.

What other players can see

Every saved beach is visible to every signed-in player on the global shore: your first name, the beach's name, its real-world location, its sand and its items. Your email address is never shown to other players. Item submissions are private during review. Beech can publish submitted assets under the submission terms. If you play the Steam version, your Steam friends can also see the name of the beach you are on through Steam Rich Presence.

What we share

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share data for advertising or tracking. Sign-in is verified with Google, Apple, or Steam, and purchases are verified with Apple, Google Play, Steam, or Xsolla — those companies handle payment and we never see card details. Store orders are paid through Stripe (which handles the card), and your name, shipping address, email, phone number and the print files go to Printful, who make and ship the order. Everything is sent over HTTPS.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app (Settings → account → delete account). That deletes your account, beaches, inventory, purchases, stars, neighbour links, gifts, and the submission records attached to your account from our database. Assets already published in the game may remain available under the submission terms. Gift-card records we issued are kept without your name attached, for accounting, and so are store orders (with the shipping details and any beach print), which we must keep for tax records.

Children

beech is not directed at children under 13. Sign-in requires a Google, Apple, or Steam account. If you are a parent and believe your child has an account, contact us at markjen@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions: markjen@gmail.com.
