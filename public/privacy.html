Privacy Policy for beech

Last updated November 23, 2026. beech is made by Jenerator Labs. Contact: markjen@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your first name, an account identifier from that provider, and — for Google sign-in only, when Google confirms it — your email address. We do not receive your Apple email address, and we never receive passwords or payment details. When you play signed in we store your saved beaches (sand, placed items, beach name, and which real-world beach it is set on), your item inventory, stars, neighbour links, gifts, and your purchase records (store, transaction ID, product). We also receive two small automatic reports from the app: error messages (up to 500 characters, with no user ID attached) and page-load timings, used only to fix bugs. Your location is used only on your device, once, if you tap "near me" in settings to pick the closest beach — it is never stored or sent to us.

What other players can see

Every saved beach is visible to every signed-in player on the global shore: your first name, the beach's name, its real-world location, its sand and its items. You can take any beach off the shore in settings, so it is reachable only by its code or as a neighbour. Your email address is never shown to other players.

What we share

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share data for advertising or tracking. Sign-in is verified with Google or Apple, and purchases are verified with Apple or Google Play — those companies handle payment and we never see card details. Everything is sent over HTTPS.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app (Settings → account → delete account). That deletes your account, beaches, inventory, purchases, stars, neighbour links, and gifts from our database. Gift-card records we issued are kept without your name attached, for accounting.

Children

beech is not directed at children under 13. Sign-in requires a Google or Apple account. If you are a parent and believe your child has an account, contact us at markjen@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions: markjen@gmail.com.
